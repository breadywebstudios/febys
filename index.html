Our story

About Feby's Kitchen

Feby's Kitchen was founded in 2011 by Feby Cachero Baguisa dela Pena — a Filipino mom who dreamt of having her own restaurant. It started as her sideline while working in the office, her officemates were the first customers, and it later grew online through social media.

After 15 years of business knowledge, product development and hard work, we now serve hundreds of customers every week — dine in, delivery, and catering for events. Our menu has evolved from traditional Filipino cuisine to Asian fusion.

Vision

We envision to expand and have branches in all Emirates in the next 3 to 4 years and be the best, no.1 Filipino Restaurant in the UAE. We also envision establishing a name that is directly associated with good food, cosiness and a dining experience worth remembering.

Mission

Our mission is to provide the best food — not only to satisfy customers' hunger and cravings, but also to provide a high quality service that gives each and every customer the feeling of being not away from home while dining with us.

Today, we offer a home-like atmosphere and serve quality, sumptuous, freshly cooked dishes from chicken, beef, seafood and desserts — home-cooked food that takes you back home, even when we are far away from it.

We provide friendly service in a relaxing ambiance, by staff and owners who truly love what they do.

“In Feby's Kitchen we give happiness and promise to serve only the very best.”
